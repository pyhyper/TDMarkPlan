# Goal

## Objective
Run 5 continuous kilometers under 32 minutes without stopping.

## Current Level
Sedentary to beginner runner (can jog 1 km before exhaustion).

## Target
Complete 5K race pace comfortably with regular heart rate recovery.

## Deadline
2026-11-09

# Current Ability
- Stamina: Low
- Recovery: Moderate
- Flexibility: Needs daily mobility work

# Weak Areas
- Calf tightness and early pacing control
- Consistency on weekend long jogs

# Constraints
- Available time: 45 minutes / session
- 4 days per week (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun)
- Keep heart rate primarily in Zone 2 to prevent injury
